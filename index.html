01Diagnosis & Opportunity

Where Gallinée stands today

Gallinée entered Spain in 2025 as Shiseido's first pharmacy skincare brand in EMEA and a pioneer in microbiome skincare. Despite strong scientific credentials, awareness remains extremely low.

Strengths
  • First microbiome skincare pioneer
  • Scientific founder credibility
  • Backed by Shiseido
  • Patented microbiome technology
  • Accessible premium pricing
Challenges
  • Low brand awareness
  • Microbiome poorly understood
  • Limited social presence
  • Complex scientific language
  • Weak cultural relevance
Why Spain? Why now?

05The Stretch Play

We're one big group. So are the brands.

The class was split into many teams across two briefs — some on Gallinée, some on Shiseido. We took Gallinée. But the sharpest strategy isn't to win our brief in isolation: Gallinée's single biggest advantage is Shiseido standing behind it. So the boldest version of this plan stops treating them as separate problems and builds one co-branded Shiseido × Gallinée pop-up — turning the parent brand's scale and trust into Gallinée's fastest route to virality.

Shiseido × Gallinée
Save the logo lockup as cobrand.png in the same folder, then reload
Instant credibilityShiseido backing is the advantage

Gallinée steps onto Shiseido's stage. The parent's reputation de-risks a young challenger and shortcuts years of brand-building in a single moment.

More for lessShared cost, bigger stage

One premium space, two brands. Prime locations, more press and a far lower cost per impression than either brand reaches alone.

Two crowds, one roomCross-audience discovery

Shiseido's prestige shoppers meet Gallinée's accessible-premium upgraders — a natural bridge between price tiers.

A halo both waysMutual lift

Gallinée gains legitimacy and reach; Shiseido gains a younger, social-first, viral edge — and a microbiome story to anchor its science.

The best ecosystems don't compete — they collaborate. Just like the skin.
